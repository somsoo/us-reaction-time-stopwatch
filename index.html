    🧮 How it measures

    • Reaction test: after a random pause of 2 to 5 seconds, the box turns green and the page notes the time using the browser’s high-resolution clock. Your reaction time is the gap until your click, tap or key press, rounded to the nearest millisecond. A set is five counted tries; the average is rounded to the nearest millisecond.
    • False starts: a press during the red pause is not counted, and the next pause gets a new random length.
    • Stop the clock: the time from Start to Stop, compared with your target. Early results show a minus sign and late ones a plus sign. Ratings: within 0.010 s Perfect, 0.050 s Excellent, 0.100 s Great, 0.250 s Good, 0.500 s Close.
    • Device delay: every measurement also includes the delay of your screen, input device and browser. It is the same for every try on one device, so compare results on the same device.

    ❓ Frequently asked questions

    How does the reaction time test work?

    Click or tap the box, then wait. After a random pause of 2 to 5 seconds it turns green, and the timer measures how many milliseconds pass before you click or tap. Five tries make a set, and your average is the fairest number to compare, because single tries vary.

    Why are my results different on another device?

    Every result includes the delay of your screen, mouse or touchscreen and browser, which can add tens of milliseconds and differs from one device to another. Compare your scores on the same device, and use a mouse or keyboard if you want the lowest delay.

    What happens if I click too soon?

    Clicking before the box turns green is a false start. It is not counted, and the pause starts again with a new random length, so guessing the timing does not help.

    How does the stop-the-clock game work?

    Press Start and try to press Stop at exactly the target time, 10.000 seconds by default. With Hide the clock turned on, the numbers disappear after 3 seconds, so you have to count in your head. Your score is how far off you were, in thousandths of a second.

    Is anything saved or sent?

    Only your personal bests are saved, in this browser. Nothing is sent to us. If you share a challenge link, it includes the score you want your friend to beat.
